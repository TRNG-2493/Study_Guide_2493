## Definition of Ready checklist

- [ ] The story is written as who / what / why.
- [ ] It delivers value to a user (it is not a technical task).
- [ ] It has at least 2 testable Given/When/Then criteria.
- [ ] Each Then has a concrete expected result.
- [ ] It is small enough to finish in one sprint.
- [ ] It has a story point estimate.
- [ ] It does not contradict the product brief (password rules, generic login error, lockout after 5 attempts).
- [ ] Open questions are written down, not guessed.
