Here is a critique of your RequirementReviewer design:

1. The output contract has no field for a suggested rewrite of each acceptance criterion. Adding a "suggested_ac" field would make the findings much more actionable.
2. R3 (quote must appear in the AC text) is case-insensitive but does not say how to handle curly versus straight quotes; models often change quotation marks, which would make R3 fail on correct findings.
3. Severity is defined in the instructions but not in the output schema description, so consumers may not know what "high" means.
4. There is no sample pair where the story contains instructions aimed at the agent (for example "ignore your rules"). Your Boundaries section covers it, but nothing tests it.
5. max_findings of 20 is too high for a human to review; consider a maximum of 5.
6. The job sentence is good but could mention that the agent also prioritises findings.
