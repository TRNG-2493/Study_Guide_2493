Feature: Shopper registration
  Visitors create a ShopFront account.

  Scenario Outline: Registration ignores letter case in the username
    Given I am on the registration page
    When I register with username "<username>"
    Then I should see the "My account" page

    Examples:
      | username  |
      | shopper   |
      | SHOPPER   |

  Scenario: Reject an email outside the test domains
    Given I am a visitor with email "mila.ortiz@gmail.com"
    When I register
    Then I should see the message "Use an example.com or example.org address"
